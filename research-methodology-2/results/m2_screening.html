Screening results: A2f, M2 and M2c against TrackTacular

None of the three runs beats the paper yet. Our new fusion module helps detection when it uses GRACE's coverage prior, but it costs tracking. The bug fix turned out to hurt scene augmentation, which points to the next experiment.

The protocol is the paper's: threshold 0.5, TrackTacular's tracker, last epoch, nothing tuned on the test frames (cell thr0.5|tt). There are 952 ground-truth detections, so one error moves a metric by about 0.105 points.

The old-order A2 sits right of A2f on every metric. All three of its MOTA seeds (91.49, 92.44, 92.65) are above A2f's 90.76, and the only code difference between them is the batch-order fix.

Where the errors come from

MOTA counts tracker misses, tracker false positives and ID switches. Detector counts come before tracking.

A2f M2 M2c

Without VGF, the tracker's false positives equal the detector's (36 and 36). With VGF they rise: 34 to 45 for M2, and 40 to 49 for M2c. TrackTacular's tracker matches each detection's predicted previous position to existing tracks, so the likely cause is VGF degrading the offset head. This has not been verified.

Is 0.5 the right threshold?

MODA over the detection threshold, computed on the test frames. This is an upper-bound check, not a reported number.

All three peak at 0.5, and M2c ties at 0.45. Threshold tuning has nothing left to give.
